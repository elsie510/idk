Rainbow Dash Energy Bowl

⏱️ TOTAL TIME 15 min
🍽️ SERVINGS 2
DIFFICULTY EASY
⭐ RATING 4.7 / 5.0

"A vibrant rainbow smoothie bowl bursting with tropical fruits — fast as a sonic rainboom!"

Ingredients

  • 11 frozen banana
  • 21/2cup frozen mango chunks
  • 31/2 cup frozen strawberries
  • 41/2 cup coconut milk
  • 5Fresh blueberries, kiwi, strawberries, mango for topping
  • 62 tbsp granola
  • 71 tsp chia seeds

Method

  • 1Place the frozen banana, mango, strawberries and coconut milk into a blender.
  • 2Blend on high until completely smooth and thick.
  • 3Pour the smoothie into a wide, shallow bowl.
  • 4Arrange the fruit toppings in colourful rainbow stripes across the bowl.
  • 5Sprinkle with granola and chia seeds.
  • 6Serve immediately for maximum rainbow freshness!
